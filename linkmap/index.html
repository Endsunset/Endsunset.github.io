Your data & iCloud

Your projects, in your iCloud.

01

Private by default

Projects you create are stored in your personal iCloud account using Apple’s CloudKit service. LinkMap does not run a separate server to store your project data.

02

Share a project with your team

Shared projects stay in their owner’s iCloud account. Collaborators access them through iCloud sharing, with permission to view or make changes as allowed by the owner.

03

Manage your data in LinkMap

Open and manage your project records inside the app. CloudKit stores app data in iCloud; these projects do not appear as folders or documents in iCloud Drive.

Use your Apple Account with iCloud enabled to access your projects. Sharing a project gives collaborators access to that project, not to the rest of your personal iCloud files.

Removing the app from a device may leave its data in iCloud. Manage project records in LinkMap, and review the privacy policy for details about storage, sharing, and deletion.
