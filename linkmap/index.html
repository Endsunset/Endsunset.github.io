Projects

Start with a project.

Build a reusable foundation of places and Items before organizing field activities.

Build your map

Organize Regions, Layers, and Locations in one Project. Give your team a shared map of the places where work happens.

Set up your Items

Define the Items your team uses across Activities. Keep a reusable catalog in the Project, ready for each new round of work.

Item definitions belong to the Project. Supply and quantities are recorded in active Activities.

iCloud

Your projects, in your iCloud.

Project data lives in its owner’s iCloud account. Apple services keep changes in sync between devices with access.

Share projects

Invite collaborators to your Project and choose who can view or edit its reusable places and Item definitions.

Share activities

Invite people separately to the Activity they need. Give them permission to help with planning or record work while the Activity is active.

Collaborators need both invitations: accept the Project first, then the Activity. An Activity invitation does not grant Project access.

Explore the Documentation or read the privacy policy for storage and sharing details.

Activities

From plans to results.

Bring your Project into a particular round of field work, from preparation to completion.

Plan

Prepare Assignments, Routes, and ordered Stops before work begins. Decide where teams will go and which resources they will need.

Record

During active work, record Item transfers, distributions at Stops, and beneficiary counts. Save what your team delivers and the people it serves.

Review

Look back at saved outcomes through usage summaries, distribution charts, and completed Activity details. See how resources were used across teams and places.

Activities move through Planning, Active, and Ended. Ended Activities keep their saved results available for review.
