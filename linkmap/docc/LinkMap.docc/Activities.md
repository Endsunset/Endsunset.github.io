# Activities

An Activity is one round of work within a Project.

Create an Activity when you are ready to plan a visit, inspection, or other task. Add Routes for the places to visit and Assignments for the people or teams doing the work. Select the Activity in Dashboard → Context to show its route on the Map.

Archive an Activity after the work is finished. The Project and its Locations remain ready for another Activity.
