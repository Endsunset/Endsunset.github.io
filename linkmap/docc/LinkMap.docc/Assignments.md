# Assignments

Group the Routes for a person or team within an Activity.

## Create an Assignment

While the Activity is in Planning and you have permission to edit it:

1. Open the Activity, then Assignments.
2. Tap the plus button.
3. Enter an Assignment name.
4. Select the Routes for the person or team. The list shows Routes that are not already assigned.
5. Tap Save.

An Assignment can include several Routes. Prepare these connections before starting the Activity; starting locks the plan.

An Assignment organizes the work. To give someone access to it, share the Project and Activity with them; see <doc:Sharing>.

## Open your work

In an active Activity, tap Assignments and choose an Assignment. Open Routes and choose a Route to see its Stops. The map shows the work you open in the Console.

## Preview and share a report

1. Open an Assignment in an active Activity.
2. Tap Preview Report.
3. Tap Copy to copy the report text, or Share to choose a destination.

Use Refresh to update the preview. Report Settings lets you adjust its presentation. You can also open Settings from Projects and choose Report Style.
