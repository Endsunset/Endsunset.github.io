# How LinkMap Works

View the places in your LinkMap Projects alongside Apple Maps search.

## Keep your places together

A Project holds the places for an area or shared effort. A Location is a saved place, such as an entrance, room, checkpoint, or delivery point. Regions group places, and Layers organize levels such as floors in a building.

The web app lets you inspect these saved records and your Project's Item names. Your private Projects and shared Projects you have already accepted are available after you sign in. See <doc:Project>.

## Search or browse

Search finds places and addresses on Apple Maps. Project opens the places saved in LinkMap. Searching for an Apple Maps place does not add it to a Project or change a saved Location.

The map displays ground-level Locations and saved areas. Locations on other levels remain available in Project lists and details; opening them does not switch the map to that level. See <doc:Map> and <doc:Regions-and-Layers>.

## Continue work in iOS

The current web app is read-only, including for Project owners and collaborators with editing permission. There are no changes to save when you browse or close details.

Use LinkMap for iOS to create or edit Projects, Locations, Regions, Layers, and Items; import Locations; manage sharing; or work with Activities, Routes, Stops, and Assignments. Supply, Item transfers, returns, Stop distribution, results, and reports are also iOS tasks. Use the Documentation link in the footer to open LinkMap for iOS.
