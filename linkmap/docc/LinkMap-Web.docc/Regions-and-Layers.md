# Regions and Layers

Browse grouped places and inspect saved levels in a Project.

## Browse a Region

1. Choose Project and select your Project.
2. Under Browse project, choose Regions, then choose a Region.
3. Choose Locations to see the Region's places, or choose a Layer to inspect its details.

A Region groups related Locations and Layers, such as the places in a building. You can also open a Region from a Location's or Layer's details.

## Inspect a Layer

1. Choose Project → Layers, or open a Layer from a Region or Location.
2. Review its Name, Level, and Location count.
3. Choose a Location in its list to inspect the saved place.
4. Use the Region link, when available, to open the group it belongs to.

The Region lists Layers from higher to lower levels, with Map · Level 0 among them. Show layer on map is also available in Layer details. The current web map remains at ground level when you choose either control; these controls do not switch to another floor or reveal its markers.

## Understand ground-level viewing

Level 0 is the ground level. The map shows Locations without a Layer and those belonging to level-zero Layers, together with their saved area outlines. Locations on higher and lower levels remain readable from Project, Region, and Layer lists.

Use LinkMap for iOS to view and edit other map levels, create Regions or Layers, or move a Location between them. Refresh the selected Project in the web app after saving your changes. See <doc:iCloud>.
