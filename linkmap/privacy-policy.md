# LinkMap Privacy Policy

- **Last Updated:** October 5, 2026
- **Effective Date:** May 31, 2025

## 1. Overview

This Privacy Policy applies to LinkMap for iOS and LinkMap for Web. It explains how LinkMap handles information when you plan places and Activities within private or shared Projects.

## 2. Information You Provide

Project data can include:

- Project names, Regions, and Layers
- Locations, including names, coordinates, notes, and map geometry
- Activities, Assignments, Routes, and Stops, including visit order and progress
- Resources, quantities, and distribution records

You or collaborators with access to a shared Project create this information. LinkMap for iOS also keeps preferences and recent navigation state on your device.

## 3. iCloud and CloudKit Storage

LinkMap stores and synchronises project data using Apple's iCloud and CloudKit services through your Apple Account. Projects you create are private by default. Shared Projects make relevant project records available to invited collaborators through CloudKit sharing.

LinkMap does not operate its own independent user-account database or server for project data. Apple handles iCloud data under its applicable terms and [privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/en-ww/).

On iOS, LinkMap uses the iCloud account in your device settings. LinkMap for Web requires a separate Apple Account / iCloud sign-in and accesses your private and accepted shared Projects through CloudKit in the browser. It can display the account name returned by Apple. The browser stores a CloudKit authentication token in local storage to restore your sign-in session across visits. You can sign out from the web app's account page or clear the site's browser storage.

## 4. Collaboration Sharing

A Project is the unit you share. Invited collaborators who accept a share can access relevant project records according to the permissions granted, including viewing or changing shared data. Sharing a Project does not share the rest of your personal iCloud files.

Manage invitations and sharing in LinkMap for iOS. Ending a share removes future access through CloudKit, but collaborators may already have viewed or copied shared information.

## 5. Location and Map Services

LinkMap uses Apple's map services on iOS and the web. Map display, place searches, and related requests may send map areas, search text, and coordinates to Apple. If you use the current-location feature, your device or browser asks for location permission. Core project planning does not require continuous background location tracking.

Apple's handling of map and location information, including regional map providers, is described in [Maps & Privacy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/data/en/apple-maps/).

## 6. Data We Do Not Sell

We do not sell your personal information or use or share LinkMap project data for advertising.

## 7. Analytics and Tracking

LinkMap does not include third-party advertising trackers. If Apple provides iOS crash reports, diagnostics, or App Store analytics to the developer, that information is subject to Apple's privacy policies and user settings.

## 8. Data Retention and Deletion

Project data remains in iCloud until deleted by you, a collaborator with permission, or Apple under its iCloud rules and account settings.

Use LinkMap for iOS to manage and delete Projects and project records. LinkMap for Web currently views Projects and Locations; it does not provide project editing or deletion. Signing out, clearing browser storage, or removing the iOS app does not delete project data in iCloud. You can also manage iCloud data through Apple Account and iCloud settings where available. See the [Documentation](../documentation/) for platform and sharing guidance.

## 9. Data Security

LinkMap relies on Apple's iCloud and CloudKit security for cloud storage and synchronisation. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

Protect access to your Apple Account, devices, browser sessions, and Project invitations.

## 10. Children's Privacy

LinkMap is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information through LinkMap, contact us so appropriate action can be taken.

## 11. International Use

Apple services may process your information in locations where Apple operates, under Apple's applicable terms and privacy policies.

## 12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy and its "Last Updated" date. Material changes may also be communicated through the LinkMap website or App Store listing.

## 13. Contact

For privacy questions, bug reports, or help managing data, contact the developer through [LinkMap support](https://github.com/Endsunset/Endsunset.github.io/issues). GitHub issues are public; do not include private project data or account credentials.
