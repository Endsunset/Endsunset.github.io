# Assignments

Group the Routes for a person or team within an Activity.

## Create an Assignment

While the Activity is in Planning and you have permission to edit it:

1. Open the Activity, then Assignments.
2. Tap the plus button.
3. Enter an Assignment name.
4. Select the Routes for the person or team. The list shows Routes that are not already assigned.
5. Tap Save.

An Assignment can include several Routes. Prepare these connections before starting the Activity; starting locks the plan.

To change a Planning Assignment, open its More menu, choose Edit, update its name or selected Routes, then Save. Routes already given to another Assignment are unavailable. Cancel leaves the saved plan unchanged.

An Assignment organizes the work. To give someone access to it, share the Project and Activity with them; see <doc:Sharing>.

## Open your work

In an active Activity, tap Assignments and choose an Assignment. Open Routes and choose a Route to see its Stops. The map shows the work you open in the Console.

## Receive, return, or transfer Items

The active Activity can transfer Items to this Assignment. Its Resources show what remains available for delivery or further transfer. Tap Record Item Transfers to return unused Items to the Activity or transfer them to another Assignment in the same Activity. See <doc:Item-Transfers> for the Review and Save steps.

At a Route's Stops, record service and delivered quantities. Deliveries use this Assignment's resources. See <doc:Routes-and-Stops>.

## Review results and share a report

1. Open an Assignment in an Active or Ended Activity.
2. Tap Preview Report.
3. Tap Copy to copy the report text, or Share to choose a destination.

Use Refresh to update the preview. Report Settings lets you adjust its presentation. You can also open Settings from Projects and choose Report Style.

Active Assignments show compact saved progress. Ended Assignments provide usage and distribution results and read-only Route details. See <doc:Results-and-Reports> for graphs, report templates, and display options.
