# LinkMap Privacy Policy

- **Last Updated:** October 5, 2026
- **Effective Date:** May 31, 2025

## 1. Overview

This policy applies to LinkMap for iOS and LinkMap for Web, developed by Endsunset.

Endsunset does not collect or store your LinkMap project data on its own servers. LinkMap has no Endsunset-operated backend or independent user-account database. It uses Apple services to store and synchronise project data, share Projects, display maps, and sign in on the web.

## 2. Data Stored in Your iCloud Account

LinkMap stores and synchronises project data through Apple's iCloud / CloudKit services. Private Projects belong to your iCloud account and are private by default. Using LinkMap does not give Endsunset access to your private project records.

Data you or your collaborators create can include:

- Project names, Regions, and Layers
- Locations, including names, coordinates, notes, and map geometry
- Activities, Assignments, Routes, and Stops, including visit order and progress
- Resources, quantities, and distribution records

LinkMap for iOS also keeps preferences and recent navigation state on your device. Apple's handling of cloud data is governed by its applicable terms and [privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/en-ww/).

## 3. Sharing and Collaboration

A Project is the unit you share. CloudKit sharing makes relevant project records available to collaborators who accept an invitation, according to the permissions granted. Sharing does not copy project data to an Endsunset server or share the rest of your personal iCloud files.

Manage invitations and sharing in LinkMap for iOS. Ending a share removes future access through CloudKit, but collaborators may already have viewed or copied shared information.

## 4. Apple Maps and Location Services

Map display, place searches, and related requests are handled through Apple's mapping services on iOS and the web. These requests may send map areas, search text, and coordinates to Apple. The current-location feature requires device or browser location permission; core project planning does not require continuous background location tracking.

Apple's handling of map and location information, including regional map providers, is described in [Maps & Privacy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/data/en/apple-maps/).

## 5. Web App and Apple Account Sign-In

On iOS, LinkMap uses the iCloud account in your device settings. LinkMap for Web requires a separate Apple Account / iCloud sign-in, handled through Apple services. The browser accesses your private and accepted shared Projects directly through CloudKit and can display the account name returned by Apple.

The web app stores a CloudKit authentication token in browser local storage to restore your session across visits. This token is kept in your browser, not in an Endsunset account database. You can sign out from the web app's account page or clear the site's browser storage.

## 6. Data Management and Deletion

Use LinkMap for iOS to manage and delete Projects and project records. LinkMap for Web currently views Projects and Locations; it does not provide project editing or deletion. Data remains in iCloud until deleted by you, a collaborator with permission, or Apple under its iCloud rules and account settings.

Signing out, clearing browser storage, or removing the iOS app does not delete project data in iCloud. You can also manage iCloud data through Apple Account and iCloud settings where available. See the [Documentation](../documentation/) for platform and sharing guidance.

Cloud storage and synchronisation rely on Apple's iCloud and CloudKit security. Protect access to your Apple Account, devices, browser sessions, and Project invitations.

## 7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy and its "Last Updated" date. Material changes may also be communicated through the LinkMap website or App Store listing.

## 8. Contact and Support

For privacy questions, bug reports, or help managing data, contact Endsunset through [LinkMap support](https://github.com/Endsunset/Endsunset.github.io/issues). Endsunset receives information you choose to include in a support request. GitHub issues are public; do not include private project data or account credentials.
