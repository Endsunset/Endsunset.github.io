# Maps

Find places in LinkMap and open a maps app when you need directions.

## Search the map

Tap Search Maps, enter a place or address, then choose a suggestion. To save a place for your team, add it as a Project Location. See <doc:Locations>.

## Change the map's appearance

Use the Map Style button in the Console toolbar. When the Console shows a Back button, open More and choose Map Style. Choose Standard, Imagery, or Hybrid.

## Open a maps app

1. Tap a Location marker to open its details.
2. Open Navigation.
3. Choose Apple Map, Google Map, or Amap.

LinkMap opens the selected service for that Location. Follow directions there. If Amap is unavailable, its App Store page opens so you can install it.

When comparing a place between map services, check that the destination marker is where you expect it to be.

## Map services and privacy

LinkMap uses Apple's map services for its map and place search. These actions may send requests to Apple. See the [privacy policy](https://endsunset.github.io/linkmap/privacy-policy/) for details.
