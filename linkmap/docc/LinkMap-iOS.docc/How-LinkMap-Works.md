# How LinkMap Works

Bring places, routes, and shared work together on a map.

## Keep your places together

A Project holds the places for an area or shared effort. Add a Location for each entrance, room, checkpoint, or delivery point your team needs to find. Use Regions to group places and Layers to organize levels, such as floors in a building.

Your Project map stays available for future visits. You can plan another round of work using the same Locations.

## Plan a visit

An Activity is one round of work in a Project. A Route puts Locations in visit order; each visit is a Stop. An Assignment groups the Routes for a person or team.

For example, keep a building's delivery points in one Project. Create an Activity for today's deliveries, arrange the Stops into Routes, and give each team an Assignment. Create reusable Items such as Meal pack, record Supply to the active Activity, transfer quantities to each team's Assignment, and record deliveries at its Stops.

Activities move through three states:

- **Planning:** Prepare the Routes and Assignments. Resource and service changes are unavailable.
- **Active:** Follow the locked plan, record Item transfers, and update service and deliveries at Stops.
- **Ended:** Review saved results, history, and reports. The Activity is read-only.

## Track resources and results

<doc:Items> defines what your team counts. <doc:Item-Transfers> explains Supply, allocations, returns, and transfers between teams. A Route uses its Assignment's resources when you record deliveries at Stops; it has no separate stock balance.

<doc:Results-and-Reports> explains saved summaries, distribution graphs, and shareable Assignment reports. Use <doc:Settings-and-Imports> for report preferences, cache controls, and importing existing Location documents.

## Work with other people

Share a Project so collaborators can access its map. Share an Activity separately when they also need access to that work. Each invitation has its own permissions. See <doc:Sharing>.

LinkMap uses your Apple Account and iCloud for your Projects. This guide describes LinkMap for iOS. Choose All Collections above for the Web App's development status.
