# LinkMap Privacy Policy

- **Last Updated:** September 16, 2026
- **Effective Date:** May 31, 2025

## 1. Overview

LinkMap ("the App", "we", "us", or "our") is an iOS app for private map-based workspace planning. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be handled when you use the App and how that information is stored, shared, and protected.

By using LinkMap, you agree to this Privacy Policy.

## 2. Information You Provide

LinkMap lets you create and manage workspace planning data, including:

- Workspace names
- Regions, layers, locations, and map geometry
- Project names and planning records
- Routes, route stops, assignments, supplies, items, and distribution records
- Photos or other assets you choose to attach to records
- App preferences and recent navigation state

This information is created by you or by collaborators who have access to a shared workspace.

## 3. iCloud and CloudKit Storage

LinkMap uses Apple's iCloud and CloudKit services to store app data.

Workspace data is stored in the user's private iCloud database by default. If you choose to share a workspace, LinkMap uses CloudKit sharing so authorized collaborators can access the shared workspace through Apple's shared CloudKit database.

We do not operate a separate server for LinkMap workspace data. iCloud data is handled by Apple under Apple's applicable iCloud terms and privacy policies.

## 4. Collaboration Sharing

When you share a workspace, the people who accept the share may be able to view, create, edit, or delete records in that shared workspace depending on the permissions provided through CloudKit sharing.

You should only share a workspace with people you trust. Ending a share removes future shared access through CloudKit, but collaborators may have already seen information that was shared with them.

## 5. Location and Map Services

LinkMap uses Apple MapKit to display maps and support map-related features. Map display, search, and related map service requests may be sent directly to Apple. Apple's handling of MapKit and location-related information is governed by Apple's privacy policies.

LinkMap does not require continuous background location tracking for its core workspace planning features.

## 6. Data We Do Not Sell

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use LinkMap workspace data for advertising. We do not share LinkMap workspace data with third-party advertisers.

## 7. Analytics and Tracking

LinkMap does not include third-party advertising trackers. If Apple provides crash reports, diagnostics, or App Store analytics to the developer, that information is provided through Apple's developer services and is subject to Apple's privacy policies and user settings.

## 8. Data Retention and Deletion

Workspace data remains in iCloud until it is deleted by you, by a collaborator with permission, or by Apple according to iCloud rules and account settings.

Deleting records or workspaces in the App removes those records from the applicable CloudKit database. Removing the App from a device may not delete data already stored in iCloud. You can manage iCloud data through Apple account and iCloud settings where available.

## 9. Data Security

LinkMap relies on Apple's iCloud and CloudKit security for cloud storage and synchronization. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

You are responsible for protecting access to your Apple Account, device, and shared workspace invitations.

## 10. Children's Privacy

LinkMap is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information through the App, contact us so appropriate action can be taken.

## 11. International Use

If you use LinkMap outside your country or region, your information may be processed through Apple services in locations where Apple operates. Apple's processing is governed by Apple's applicable terms and privacy policies.

## 12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When changes are made, we will update the "Last Updated" date. Material changes may also be communicated through the App Store listing or other appropriate means.

Your continued use of LinkMap after a policy update means you accept the updated policy.
