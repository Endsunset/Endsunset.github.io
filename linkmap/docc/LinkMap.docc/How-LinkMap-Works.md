# How LinkMap Works

Bring places, routes, and shared work together on a map.

## Keep your places together

A Project holds the places for an area or shared effort. Add a Location for each entrance, room, checkpoint, or delivery point your team needs to find. Use Regions to group places and Layers to organize levels, such as floors in a building.

Your Project map stays available for future visits. You can plan another round of work using the same Locations.

## Plan a visit

An Activity is one round of work in a Project. A Route puts Locations in visit order; each visit is a Stop. An Assignment groups the Routes for a person or team.

For example, keep a building's delivery points in one Project. Create an Activity for today's deliveries, arrange the Stops into Routes, and give each team an Assignment.

Activities move through three states:

- **Planning:** Prepare the Routes and Assignments.
- **Active:** Follow the plan. Its Routes and Assignments are locked.
- **Ended:** Review the work. The Activity is read-only.

See <doc:Basic-Workflow> to try this in the iOS app.

## Work with other people

Share a Project so collaborators can access its map. Share an Activity separately when they also need access to that work. Each invitation has its own permissions. See <doc:Sharing>.

LinkMap uses your Apple Account and iCloud for your Projects. This guide describes LinkMap for iOS; see <doc:Web-Platform> for the Web App's development status.
