# iCloud

Use your Apple Account to keep your LinkMap Projects available across your devices.

## Get ready to use iCloud

LinkMap for iOS uses the Apple Account signed in on your device. Make sure iCloud is available in Settings, then open LinkMap. You do not need a separate LinkMap account.

Projects you create are private until you share them. Shared Projects and Activities use their owner's invitations and permissions; see <doc:Sharing>.

## If your account is unavailable

Follow the message shown by LinkMap. Check your Apple Account in Settings and your network connection, then retry. If iCloud access is restricted on the device, resolve that restriction before continuing.

A temporary network problem may still let you open locally available information. Reconnect when you need to load or save shared changes. Check any save error and retry before relying on a change being available to others.

## Refresh work and recover a save

1. Check the network banner and any error message. Reconnect if the device is offline.
2. Pull down the Projects, Activities, Assignments, or Routes list to refresh it. Use Project Map → Refresh Map Data for places.
3. If a detail or overview shows Unable to Load Details or Unable to Load Overview, tap Retry. A failed load does not mean the Project has no work.
4. For a failed Item transfer save, review the retained draft against refreshed quantities, then Review and Save again. See <doc:Item-Transfers>.
5. For a failed Stop update, correct the retained change and Retry, or Discard it. Wait for the confirmed total before leaving. See <doc:Routes-and-Stops>.

If the owner changed sharing permissions or started or ended an Activity, refresh and check your current invitation and Activity state. Changes permitted in a previous state may no longer be available. See <doc:Sharing>.

Keep the app open while resolving an unsaved Stop edit; these drafts do not survive a relaunch.

## Manage your data

Manage Projects and their records in LinkMap. Removing the app may leave its data in iCloud. Read the [privacy policy](https://endsunset.github.io/linkmap/privacy-policy/) for storage, sharing, and deletion details.

Use the Documentation link in the footer for the Web App's current status.
