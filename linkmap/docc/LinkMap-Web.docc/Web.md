Explore your saved places and search Apple Maps in your browser.

## Get ready

[Open the Web App](https://endsunset.github.io/linkmap/app/) with JavaScript enabled and an internet connection. Search Maps works without signing in. To view your Projects, choose Project and sign in with the Apple Account you use for LinkMap. See <doc:iCloud>.

The web app is under development and currently provides read-only viewing. Create and edit Projects, accept invitations, plan Activities, and record work in LinkMap for iOS. Use the Documentation link in the footer to open the iOS guide for those tasks.

## Start with your map

Choose Search to find a place or address, or Project to open saved Locations, Regions, Layers, and Items. Follow <doc:Basic-Workflow> for a first example, or choose a topic below.

Help in the app opens this guide in a new tab, leaving your map available.
