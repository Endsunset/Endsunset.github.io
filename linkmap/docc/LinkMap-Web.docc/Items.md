# Items

Look up the Item names saved in a Project.

## Browse Items

1. Choose Project and select your Project.
2. Under Browse project, choose Items. You can also open Items from Project details.
3. Choose an Item to see its saved Name.
4. Choose Back to return to the list, or Close details to dismiss it.

An Item names something your team counts, such as Meal pack. The web app currently shows Item names only. It does not show quantities, balances, Supply, transfers, returns, or deliveries.

## Manage resources in iOS

Use LinkMap for iOS to create Items, record Supply and Item transfers in an active Activity, record returns, or distribute Items at Stops. Results and reports are also available in iOS. Use the Documentation link in the footer to open the iOS guide and its Items, Item Transfers, Routes and Stops, and Results and Reports topics.

After adding an Item in iOS, choose Project → Refresh map data to update the web list. See <doc:iCloud>.
